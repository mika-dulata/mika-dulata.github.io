The hardest crypto tickets
can’t be undone.

A wrong-network send. A drained wallet. A fake support agent asking for a recovery phrase.

Support can’t reverse every loss. It can stop the next one, collect useful evidence, and tell the customer the truth. That’s the standard I bring to support operations, quality assurance, and escalation processes.

I lead coaching, service levels, reporting, and cross-functional escalations. My experience spans support and internal audit at Binance, with an earlier foundation in banking, credit control, and billing.
