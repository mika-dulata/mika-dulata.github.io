    How I work

    Five rules I keep coming back to

    Short enough to remember at 3 a.m., when a drain is in progress and the queue is full.

    1. i

      Money at risk, a person replies first.

      If the user thinks their funds are in danger, a human takes it. Everything else can be automated.

    2. ii

      No cold handoffs.

      The agent who picks up already knows what the user told the bot. Nobody repeats their story.

    3. iii

      Containment is not resolution.

      I report verified resolution and repeat contacts next to it, or the number lies.

    4. iv

      Every article has an owner and a review date.

      The bot is only as good as the knowledge base it reads.

    5. v

      Compliance-aware, not compliance-scary.

      Support follows KYC and AML rules and still sounds like a person.
